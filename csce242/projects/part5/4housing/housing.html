Finding Roommates

  • Ask friends, classmates, teammates, or people in your major if they are looking for housing.
  • Check with your university's student services for roommate matching programs.
  • Use online platforms and social media groups dedicated to finding roommates.
  • Attend housing fairs and events where you can meet potential roommates.
  • Consider reaching out to residents in your desired housing complex.
