Business Casual

  • Blouses, knit tops, sweaters, or modest professional shirts
  • Dress pants, slacks, ankle pants, or a knee-length/midi skirt
  • A casual dress that is not too short, tight, or low-cut
  • Closed-toe flats, loafers, clean dress shoes, or low heels
  • A blazer, cardigan, or light jacket if you want an extra-professional look

Business Professional

  • A matching pantsuit or skirt suit in neutral colors like black, navy, gray, or beige
  • A polished blouse or button-up shirt under the blazer
  • Dress pants or a knee-length/midi pencil or A-line skirt
  • Closed-toe pumps, flats, or low heels in a neutral color
  • Minimal jewelry and a structured handbag or tote
