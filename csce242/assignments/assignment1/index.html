Columbia, SC Welcomes You

Columbia, South Carolina, is cutely nicknamed "Soda City" due to it's original abbreviation of "Cola". It is a vibrant town and home to the state capitol, garenteeing a deep history. The University of South Carolina is located in the heart of the city, and is home to the Gamecocks. This town welcomes people home from all over with its southern charm and modern culture.

Columbia is home to much more than the Gamecocks, including a rich cultural scene and beautiful natural surroundings. You can spend your day at the riverwalk, or the nearby zoo and botanical gardens. If you are interested in the city life, columbia has a great main street which is home to all sorts of food and activities like the Soda City Market! Stop by and see what our city has to offer!
